Settings

Smaller groups are less regulated and far less likely to decline Page posts. Groups with no recent activity (e.g. dead since 2020) are skipped entirely.

Developer mode removes the built-in safety rails. Read the warnings below before enabling — Facebook can flag accounts that join too many groups or post too aggressively.

Delays are the random wait between posts. The soft cap pauses posting for the rest of the day once reached.

Join Safety

HARD STOP (default): the app will never join more than 10 groups per day. This permanent safety cap protects your account from a Facebook "joining too fast" restriction. When it's reached, the app stops, saves progress, and tells you to run Auto-Join again the next day to continue. Developer mode disables this cap.

Join delays are the random pause between each group join (30–90s recommended). Longer, randomized pauses look more human and are safer.
